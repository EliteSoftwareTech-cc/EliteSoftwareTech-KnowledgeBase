# Stage 1 Rollout Notes: Live Portals & Subdomain Architecture

**Published:** 2026-11-20 • **Author:** EliteSoftware Tech Co. | TheShadyRainbow4 | {Zach}

Today marks the first wave of standalone project portals going live under `*.elitesoftwaretech.cc` alongside the redesigned main hub at [`main.elitesoftwaretech.cc`](https://main.elitesoftwaretech.cc/).

## Currently Live Project Portals
- **[PidgeWin32 (`pidgewin32.elitesoftwaretech.cc`)](https://pidgewin32.elitesoftwaretech.cc/):** A heavily redesigned fork of Windhawk aimed specifically at Windows 10 and older (with no Windows 11 mods), supporting both `.pw32.cpp` and `.wh.cpp` mods plus the online [Mod Gallery](https://pidgewin32-gallery.elitesoftwaretech.cc/).
- **[EliteHotkey Service (`elitehotkeyservice.elitesoftwaretech.cc`)](https://elitehotkeyservice.elitesoftwaretech.cc/):** Unified portal covering both the current named-pipe/registry `Elite_Hot-Key_Service` and the v1 Legacy Edition (`v1.1.1.0`).
- **[WinNTMU & Packs (`winntmu.elitesoftwaretech.cc`)](https://winntmu.elitesoftwaretech.cc/):** Combined portal for `Windows_NT_Modding_Utility-FORK` (`v2.17.27.27`) and 8 Vista restoration packs.
- **[Icon Maker (`icon-maker.elitesoftwaretech.cc`)](https://icon-maker.elitesoftwaretech.cc/):** Native C++ Win32 multi-resolution `.ico` editor and converter (`1.2.5.0`).
- **[EliteSoftware Build Automations (`elitesoftware-build-automations.elitesoftwaretech.cc`)](https://elitesoftware-build-automations.elitesoftwaretech.cc/):** 40+ native C++ CLI build, signing, PE icon/checksum, and packaging utilities (`v1.0.2.6`).

Pending subdomains currently route cleanly to [`coming-soon.elitesoftwaretech.cc`](https://coming-soon.elitesoftwaretech.cc/) until each repository finishes its documentation, screenshots, and release packaging.
